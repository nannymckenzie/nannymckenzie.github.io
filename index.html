Bringing my baby

My little one (born May 2, 2026) will join us most days, creating a small mixed-age environment where children learn and grow together. My approach

How I care for children

Play-based learning

Children do their best learning through play. Our days include projects, art, stories, music, and time outside, always following your child’s curiosity.

Relationship-centered care

Trusting relationships come first. I love connecting meaningfully with each child, keep communication with parents warm and open, and build routines that feel safe and predictable.

Developmentally appropriate practice

With my teaching background, I meet each child exactly where they are at, with realistic expectations and activities that stretch their skills gently, never pressuring them.

What I offer

Day-to-day

  • Full-time nanny care in Whatcom County, at your home and/or at my home.
  • Age-appropriate activities focused on development of curiosity, creativity, problem-solving, and motor skills.
  • Strong communication. Daily check-ins along with optional photo and videos sent to you during the day.
  • Light child-related tidying. Play spaces reset, children's dishes done, and little messes cleaned along the way.
  • Screen-free fun. Lots of reading, dancing and exploring.
  • Language development. Intentional language development in English and Spanish (optional).
  • Healthy nutrition. Optional: I shop, purchase, and prep your child’s homemade plant-based lunches & snacks M-F for a small weekly fee.

The right fit matters

We may be a great fit if you...

  • Value play-based learning and time outdoors over screens
  • Are excited about a warm mixed-age environment, with my little one joining most days
  • Want full-time, long-term care with one consistent, professional caregiver
  • Appreciate open, honest communication between parents and caregiver

We may not be the right match if you...

  • Need care with no other children present
  • Are looking for occasional babysitting or part-time hours
  • Need a highly variable or on-call schedule week to week
  • Prefer a caregiver who focuses on housekeeping beyond the children's needs
