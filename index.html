About

From the classroom to your living room

With four years experience teaching primary grades (K-2) and three years experience as a full-time nanny, I bring the expertise of an educator and the nurturing, individualized approach of a dedicated caregiver. I'm passionate about creating meaningful relationships with children and believe early childhood education is the cornerstone in building our future.

I studied at Mount Holyoke College, earned my Washington State teaching certification, and taught elementary school here in Whatcom County before spending two years teaching at a bilingual school in Mexico. Working with children across ages, languages, and cultures taught me that kids thrive when the adults around them are calm, consistent, and genuinely delighted by who they are.

I'm passionate about play-based learning and nature exploration, and our days together will be full of both.

Bringing my baby

Care that comes with a built-in friend

My little one will join us most days, creating a small mixed-age environment where children learn and grow together. Drawing on my experience in mixed-age classrooms and nanny shares, I provide individualized, developmentally appropriate care while intentionally fostering empathy, communication, and sense of belonging in community.

It feels less like drop-off care and more like growing up alongside a trusted family. Excited to discuss details!

My approach

How I care for your child

Play-based learning

Children do their best learning through play. Our days include open-ended projects, art, stories, music, and plenty of time outside in nature, always following the children's curiosity.

Relationship-centered care

Trusting relationships come first. I take time to really know each child, keep communication with parents warm and open, and build routines that feel safe and predictable.

Developmentally appropriate practice

My teaching background helps me meet each child exactly where they are, with realistic expectations and activities that stretch them gently, never pressure them.

Ideal family

The right match matters to me too

I'm looking for a family who values open communication and a warm, collaborative relationship. If you want thoughtful, dependable care where your child feels connected to community and inspired to learn and grow, I'd love to connect!

We may be a great fit if you...

  • Value play-based learning and time outdoors over screens and rigid academics

Common questions

Answers, up front

Will your little one really be with you every day?

Most days, yes. This is a genuine benefit for your child: a built-in friend and a small mixed-age environment where kids naturally practice sharing, empathy, and communication. My attention stays firmly on the children in my care, and my teaching experience in mixed-age classrooms means I know how to make it work beautifully.

What area do you serve?

I'm based in Bellingham's York Neighborhood and happy to talk with families throughout Whatcom County. Care can happen at your home and/or at my home. If you're a bit farther out, reach out anyway and we can see what makes sense.

Do you offer a trial period?

Yes. I like to start with a paid trial period so your family and I can make sure the fit feels right on both sides before we commit long term.

What happens when someone gets sick?

Clear communication, both ways. If your child or mine is contagious, we let each other know early and follow a simple sick policy we agree on up front, so nobody is guessing.

What schedule are you looking for?

I'm looking for a full-time weekday arrangement, Monday through Friday, starting as early as Fall 2026. Exact hours are flexible within reason, and we can settle on the rhythm that works for your family when we talk.
